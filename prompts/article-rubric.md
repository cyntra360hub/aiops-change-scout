# Article Rubric

Before an article is generated, the finding must satisfy:

1. Something actually changed.
2. The change is supported by a primary or reputable source.
3. The agent identified an operational implication rather than merely repeating the change.
4. The implication affects a defined practitioner audience.
5. There is enough evidence to explain why the finding matters.
6. The article contains a specific insight, not a generic summary.
7. The article does not exist merely because the calendar says it is time to publish.

Reject internally if:
- significance score < configured threshold
- only one obvious announcement exists and there is no analysis
- the finding is promotional
- evidence is insufficient
- the article could have been generated without doing the investigation
